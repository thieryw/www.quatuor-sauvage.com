The Quartet

Music in all its variety.

Founded in April 2022, Quatuor Sauvage is a French string quartet based between Paris and Montpellier. The ensemble grew out of a wish to set the great string quartet repertoire in conversation with new music, cross-disciplinary projects and concert formats that open the door to new audiences.

Its name is a tribute to Cécile Sauvage, poet and mother of Olivier Messiaen, whose singular and forceful literary world continues to shape the quartet’s thinking.

Convinced that a quartet is built through the great repertoire — from Haydn, Mozart and Beethoven to Debussy and Ravel — the ensemble has worked with Raphaël Merlin, Christophe Coin and the Quatuor Debussy. Alongside this it commits itself to new music: Corentin Boissier’s First Quartet op. 34b, written in support of Ukraine, premiered and recorded at Studio Sextan in 2023; Alexandre Ouzounoff’s La Boussole et l’Astrolabe with oboist Stéphane Goyeau; Nicolas Dedieu’s Tristesses de la lune.

Drawn to concert formats that change the way an audience listens, the quartet self-produced an evening at the Péniche Anako in 2025, pairing Beethoven’s Quartet op. 74 “Harp” with Marc Mellits’s Tapas, followed by a participatory second half in which musicians from the audience joined the players on stage. It also performs in the immersive show Dans les Rêves de Tim Burton. That same year it was awarded second prize at the Concours Léopold Bellan, with a special mention for the first movement of Alberto Ginastera’s String Quartet no. 1.

Its current projects centre on the repertoire of women composers — Teresa Carreño’s string quartet foremost among them — on new works built from traditional Occitan melodies, and on commissions inspired by the poems of Cécile Sauvage.

Fabian Ishibashi

Violin

Born in Munich in 1993, Fabian Ishibashi discovered the violin as a child in the conservatoire where his mother worked, before moving to Paris in 1998. He studied with Richard Schmoucler, Marie Charvet, Nicolas Miribel and Christophe Poiget, and came to the quartet repertoire in 2005 in Pascal Robault’s class, later with Emmanuel Haratyk of the Quatuor Manfred. His curiosity has taken him from tango, studied with Juan José Mosalini, to the traditional musics of the Mediterranean through Rachid Brahim-Djelloul’s Middle Eastern music course. He has been leader of Pixelophonia, an orchestra devoted to video game music, since 2019, and joined Quatuor Sauvage in 2022.

Hélia Fassi

Violin

Hélia Fassi began the violin at five, near Paris. After specialist studies with Florin Szigeti, a postgraduate year at the Paris CRR with Suzanne Gessner and a master’s at the Paris Conservatoire (CNSMDP) with Olivier Charlier and Joanna Matkovska, she turned towards chamber music — including contemporary repertoire with electronics, under Hae-Sun Kang. As a member of the Quatuor Gabriële, first prize at the Concours Bellan in 2021, she studied with François Salques, the Quatuor Diotima, Pro Quartet and Le Dimore del Quartetto. She joined Quatuor Sauvage in 2022.

Carolina Ortiz Romero

Viola

Carolina Ortiz Romero began playing string quartets in 2005 within El Sistema in her native Venezuela, before continuing her training at the conservatoires of Toulouse and Paris, where she was awarded two chamber music prizes with the jury’s congratulations. A founding member of the Quatuor Naos, coached by the Quatuor Ysaÿe, she played at Wigmore Hall opening for the Quatuor Ébène. A former member of the Paris Opera Academy, she appears regularly at the Palais Garnier and the Opéra Bastille, and plays as principal viola with the Orchestre Pasdeloup. Since 2024 she has taught at Chetham’s School of Music in Manchester.

William Thiery

Cello

William Thiery began the cello at ten at the Montpellier conservatoire, in the classes of Cyrille Tricoire and then Yves Potrel, where he was awarded prizes in both cello and chamber music. He continued at the Paris CRR with Hélène Dautry, alongside studies with Alexandre Lacour. He has worked with the Orchestre des Pays de la Loire, the Schola Chamber Orchestra and the Schauspielhaus Bochum, and has appeared at La Folle Journée de Nantes, the Cordes et Pics festival in Haute-Savoie and the Cordes Hérault festival. He is a founding member of Quatuor Sauvage.
